Privacy Policy

Established: 28 August 2026　Last revised: 18 September 2026
This is an English translation provided for convenience. The app itself is available in Japanese only. If there is any discrepancy between this translation and the Japanese original, the Japanese version prevails.
Yasuaki Miyaba (trading as "miyax", hereinafter "we" or "us") sets out below the privacy policy (the "Policy") governing how we handle information about users (the "User") of the mobile application "Onstreak" (the "App").

1. Basic principles

The App is designed to be usable without registering an account (no name, email address or similar is required). Habit data (habit names, icons, streak counts, personal bests and the like, the "Habit Data") is in principle stored locally on the device. The App may make incidental use of the backup facilities built into the operating system (iCloud / Google account), but these are mechanisms provided by the respective operating systems and are not data we collect directly.

Only where a User wishes to transfer their data to a new device do we temporarily store data on an external cloud service (Google Firebase), by means of the "transfer code" feature described below.

2. Information we obtain

2.1 Data stored when a transfer code is issued (optional feature)

Only when the User taps "Issue a code" on the settings screen do we temporarily store the following information on Firebase Firestore, associated with that code.

  • Ad-removal flag (whether the Light Plan or Full Plan has been purchased)
  • The list of unlocked theme colours
  • The record for each habit (habit name, icon, current streak, personal best)

The code is deleted automatically 24 hours after it is issued, after which it can no longer be used to gain access. We do not re-verify purchase receipts; we copy the flags and data above as they are.

2.2 Information obtained automatically (for serving advertisements)

On the free and Light plans, so that the App can be offered free of charge, we use a third-party advertising service (Google AdMob). That service may automatically obtain information such as the following in order to serve advertisements and measure their effectiveness.

  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA). On iOS the IDFA is used only where you have granted permission through ATT; see below)
  • Device information (OS version, device model and the like)
  • Approximate location (country or region level)
  • Information about how the App is used

The App uses a consent management platform (UMP: User Messaging Platform) to display a consent screen to users in the relevant regions. Whether the screen is shown is determined automatically according to the User's location.

  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland: a consent screen is shown asking whether personal data may be used for advertising. The User may choose not to consent
  • Users in certain U.S. states: a screen is shown allowing the User to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Users elsewhere: these screens are not shown

A choice once made can be changed at any time from "Advertising consent settings" on the App's settings screen (this item appears only for users in the regions listed above).

On iOS, the App presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorisation request. This is the system prompt asking whether the App may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. It is shown once, the first time you use the App.

  • If you allow tracking: the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and the advertisements shown to you become more relevant
  • If you do not allow tracking: the IDFA is not used and no such tracking takes place. Advertisements are still shown, but they are less relevant

You can change this choice at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Please also refer to Google AdMob's privacy policy regarding the handling of this information.

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Once the Full Plan (complete removal of advertisements) has been purchased, advertising is stopped entirely and the collection of this information stops with it.

2.3 Information relating to purchases

In-app purchases (the Light Plan, the Full Plan and the additional theme colours) are processed through the payment systems of the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not directly obtain or store payment information such as credit card numbers. We confirm whether a purchase has completed solely through the purchase-verification mechanisms provided by each platform.

3. Purposes for which information is used

We use the information we obtain solely for the following purposes.

  1. To provide the functions of the App (recording habits, displaying statistics and the like)
  2. To transfer data to a new device, where the User has asked for this
  3. To serve advertisements and measure their effectiveness (free and Light plans only)
  4. To investigate faults and improve the App

4. Provision to third parties

Except where required by law, we do not provide the information we obtain to third parties without the consent of the person concerned. However, for the purposes set out in the preceding section, we may entrust the handling of information to the following providers.

  • Advertising provider (Google AdMob)
  • Cloud infrastructure provider (Google Firebase)

5. Retention periods

  • Temporary data stored when a transfer code is issued (2.1): deleted 24 hours after issue, or when the transfer completes, whichever is earlier
  • Local data on the device: retained until the User uninstalls the App or deletes it within the App

6. Rights of users

  • The User may disable the App's notification permission and advertising personalisation settings at any time from the device settings
  • Users in the regions described in "2.2 Information obtained automatically (for serving advertisements)" may change their consent or opt-out choice at any time from "Advertising consent settings" on the App's settings screen
  • The User may delete the local data on the device by uninstalling the App
  • Where a transfer code has been issued, that code expires automatically after 24 hours, after which it can no longer be used to gain access

7. Users in the EU and elsewhere outside Japan

The App may be used by users outside Japan. Where laws outside Japan apply, such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we respond as follows.

  • Legal basis for processing: consent for advertising and analytics; performance of a contract or legitimate interests for the minimum processing necessary to provide the App's functions
  • Rights of data subjects: in addition to "6. Rights of users", data subjects have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. To exercise them, please contact us at "9. Contact"
  • International data transfers: in connection with the use of Google Firebase, Google AdMob and similar services, information may be processed on servers outside the EU (in the United States and elsewhere). These providers have put appropriate safeguards in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
The consent and opt-out mechanisms responding to the GDPR and to U.S. state laws are already in place, as described in "2.2 Information obtained automatically (for serving advertisements)". Other additional measures, such as whether an EU representative needs to be appointed, will be considered in line with how the App is rolled out outside Japan.

8. Use by minors

The App is not directed at any particular age group. Where a minor uses the App, please do so with the consent of a parent or guardian. The App is not designed primarily for children under 13.
