Frequently asked questions

How do I move my data to a new device?

  1. On your current device, open Settings → "機種変更・データ引き継ぎ" (Device change / data transfer) → "コードを発行する" (Issue a code). A 12-character code is shown.
  2. Install the App on your new device, open the same screen, enter the code and tap "このコードで引き継ぐ" (Transfer with this code).

A code stops working 24 hours after it is issued and becomes invalid once it has been used. The habits and records on the new device are replaced with the transferred data. You can transfer between iPhone and Android.

How do I use the same habits on my iPhone and iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices with iCloud available, and your habits sync automatically. Synced data is encrypted on the device before it is saved to your own iCloud.

How do I delete the data saved in iCloud?

Open Settings and tap "iCloudのデータを削除" (Delete iCloud data) at the bottom (iPhone and iPad signed in to iCloud only). The habits and records on that device are deleted together with the iCloud data. Your purchases and settings are kept.

How do I use a plan I bought on a new device or after reinstalling?

Open Settings → "プラン" (Plan) → "購入を復元" (Restore purchases). You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you used to buy it.

I am not receiving reminder notifications

Check that notifications for the App are allowed in your device's Settings app. Notifications may not appear while a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is on. You can also check the permission in the App under Settings → "通知" (Notifications).
